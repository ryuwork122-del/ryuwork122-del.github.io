About

I build things that think, ship and look good.

I'm a developer and creative technologist who works across the full arc of a product. On the engineering side that means AI and machine learning, AI automation, full-stack web applications and the data analysis that tells you what is actually working.

On the creative side it means graphic design, video editing in DaVinci Resolve and game development in Godot — so the work doesn't stop at a working prototype. It gets finished, explained and put in front of people.

I like problems that need more than one discipline: a model that needs an interface, a dataset that needs a story, a game that needs a shader and a trailer. If it can be automated, I will automate it. If it can be measured, I will measure it.
